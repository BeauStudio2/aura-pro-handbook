Work coordination and execution

Carry an authorized choice into physical work and follow it through handoffs.

On this pageInputs and outputsApproachHow it worksTechnology choicesIntegration and native ownershipWhere this capability is usedConnected capabilities

Inputs and outputs

Inputs
An accepted scope, responsible people, work instructions, prerequisites, and authority.
Expected output
Assigned work, a visible exception or completion report, and a handoff to the person who checks it.

Across the architecture

Construction tools, data ownership, and migration ↗

Approach

Make every action carry an owner, scope, prerequisite, due condition, and agreed completion check. Model instructions issued, acknowledged, performed, and accepted as separate facts. Keep handoffs open across shift changes.

How it works

After approval, the superintendent assigns the two-panel hold to the foreman and the lift reservation to the lift director. The crew acknowledges the current revision. If a later change supersedes it, affected crews receive a replacement and stale instructions are marked. Unrelated work proceeds. Capture a field completion report and route the required check.

Technology choices

Keep assignments and command history in the existing app and PostgreSQL. Use a transactional outbox and retrying worker for notifications and agreed Procore handoffs. Capture field results through Raken or a Aura Pro mobile web form with offline drafts. A durable workflow engine can replace the simple worker when long-running timers, retries, and compensating actions justify it. Procore webhook behavior · Raken–Procore sync

Integration and native ownership

Start by linking into the tools crews already check. Move briefing, acknowledgment, and exception follow-up into Aura Pro one team at a time. Test lost acknowledgment, offline submission, and a stale instruction arriving after its replacement. A notification delivery alone cannot close the assignment.
