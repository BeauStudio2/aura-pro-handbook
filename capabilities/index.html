The first client application

On a small repair, one person can describe the problem, do the work, and check the result. On a development, the field crew, scheduler, and project executive each need a different part of the same decision. The chapters use that setting to explain what each part of Aura Pro contributes.

The construction client already uses Raken for field reports, Procore for project decisions, and Sage for accounting. The proposed design connects a finding in the field to the plan, the person authorized to act, and the instruction the crew receives. A workflow can move into Aura Pro when the client designates it as the place to do and record that work.
