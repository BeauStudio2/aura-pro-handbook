Reasoning and inquiry

Which question could change the next decision, and how can it be answered in time?

Choose which question deserves attention and which method can answer it.

In Mission Logic: AP identifies which unresolved question could change a decision and proposes a useful way to answer it. The responsible person directs the inquiry, with AP operating within the role the team has granted.

Inputs

Expected output
A next move, the decision it serves, and the evidence or judgment still owed.

Approach

Treat each open question as a decision someone needs to make. Record the alternatives it distinguishes, the person who needs the answer, the latest useful time, and the cost of waiting. Allow a low-effort relevance check when a finding's significance is unclear. Compare competing claims on the same crew or review window.

How it works

The one-bay sequence depends on clearance. Nest that question under the occupancy decision. Switch from analogy to measurement when the supplier identifies a different lifting frame. When the answer comes back, update the relevant alternatives and preserve the option of generating a new one. An owner changing the occupancy goal reframes the parent and can retire the child.

Technology choices

AP keeps questions and their dependencies in relational tables. Explicit decision rules and an evidence-linked reviewer interface support the inquiry. Core services run retrieval, draft extraction, or optional language-model recommendations through a provider-independent request and result format. A deterministic comparison or a human planner can supply the same recommendation. Procore supplies the formal question and the designated reviewer’s answer.

Integration and native ownership

AP assembles a decision packet around the client’s existing request for information (RFI). As the team transfers inquiry management, AP manages internal questions and links cases requiring formal correspondence to the project register. Replay cases where “wait” was sound, an inexpensive check settled a finding, or an irrelevant dimension was requested. Score whether the choice would have changed before the decision expired.
