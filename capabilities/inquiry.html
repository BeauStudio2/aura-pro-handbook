Reasoning and inquiry

Choose which question deserves attention and which method can answer it.

Inputs and outputs

Inputs
Open obligations, current evidence, possible actions, available time, and the cost of being wrong.
Expected output
A next move, the decision it serves, and the evidence or judgment still owed.

Across the architecture

Construction tools, data ownership, and migration ↗

Approach

Treat each open question as a decision someone needs to make. Record the alternatives it distinguishes, the person who needs the answer, the latest useful time, and the cost of waiting. Allow a low-effort relevance check when a finding's significance is unclear. Compare competing claims on the same crew or review window.

How it works

The one-bay sequence depends on clearance. Nest that question under the occupancy decision. Switch from analogy to measurement when the supplier identifies a different lifting frame. When the answer comes back, update the relevant alternatives and preserve the option of generating a new one. An owner changing the occupancy goal reframes the parent and can retire the child.

Technology choices

Keep questions and their dependencies in relational tables. Start with explicit decision rules and an evidence-linked reviewer interface. Run retrieval, draft extraction, or optional language-model recommendations in core behind a provider-independent request and result format. A deterministic comparison or a human planner can supply the same recommendation. Procore supplies the formal question and the designated reviewer’s answer.

Integration and native ownership

First assemble a decision packet around the client's existing request for information (RFI). Then let Aura Pro manage internal inquiries and link the cases requiring formal correspondence to the project register. Replay cases where “wait” was sound, an inexpensive check settled a finding, or an irrelevant dimension was requested. Score whether the choice would have changed before the decision expired.
