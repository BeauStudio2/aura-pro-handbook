Resources and logistics

Make the needed people, materials, equipment, and access available together.

On this pageInputs and outputsApproachHow it worksTechnology choicesIntegration and native ownershipWhere this capability is usedConnected capabilities

Inputs and outputs

Inputs
Task prerequisites, lead times, resource capacity, delivery evidence, and reservations.
Expected output
A readiness assessment that distinguishes a promised resource from one available for use.

Across the architecture

Construction tools, data ownership, and migration ↗

Approach

A task becomes ready when the correct item, people, equipment, space, and permissions are available together. Model a promise, reservation, confirmed delivery, and observed availability as distinct events. Release or amend reservations when a sequence changes.

How it works

The supplier confirms shipment; the receiving team checks the framed unit's identity and dimensions. The lift director reserves one crane window. A late delivery can invalidate that combination even when labor is booked. Recompute readiness and offer resequencing before crews mobilize. Carry the actual cost reference forward after posting.

Technology choices

Map Procore procurement/change references and the confirmed Sage job and cost-code structure into Aura Pro IDs. Use transactional capacity reservations and an event queue. A reviewed supplier CSV plus a local reservation calendar is a workable alternative to live interfaces. For congested shared equipment, reuse the planner's constraint solver rather than building a second allocator. Procore change events · Procore–Sage 300 CRE guide · OR-Tools resource scheduling

Integration and native ownership

Begin with read-only availability and alerts in Aura Pro. Take over reservations for a named resource when the superintendent agrees which calendar wins and other schedulers consume its updates. Test concurrent bookings and re-delivered supplier messages. One slot must have one effective commitment; earlier shipping promises remain traceable.
