General planning and simulation

A restorer opens the dash of a ’67 Chevy and finds a wiring change the owner’s father made. Returning the dash to factory condition would remove his work. Aura Pro shows the owner which steps would remove the modification. The restorer pauses removal at the dash while the rest of the restoration continues. Once the owner gives the instruction to keep the modification, the restorer resumes the wiring with it in place. The ’67 Chevy example

Approach

Start with what is known today and what the owner will accept as finished. Compare feasible ways to get there under the same assumptions. If one unknown could change the choice, find out who can settle it and when the answer is needed. Keep the agreed plan, the forecast, and the actual outcome distinct.

Keep the quantity, productivity rate, and crew calendar as separate planning inputs. In the illustrative Galleria estimate, an addendum adding 50 cubic yards at 0.8 labor-hours per cubic yard adds 40 labor-hours of placement. Whether opening day moves depends on crew availability, the pour sequence, and time available before the next dependent task. The planner checks those conditions before revising the date.

How it works

For October 1 occupancy, trace commissioning back to air-handler delivery and enclosure. Evaluate one reserved panel, two reserved panels, and sectional assembly against shared crane time, weather exposure, and testing availability. A field measurement invalidates one option. Run the full sequence again with that fact; identify the controlling dependency and who owns the choice.

Technology choices

Use an exchangeable solver interface with calendar, capacities, predecessors, quantities, and dated inputs. Begin with CPU discrete-event simulation in SimPy and constraint scheduling in OR-Tools. Record scenario version, random seed, assumptions, and forecast range. For a proposed lift, compare the surveyed opening with the dimensions of the rigged load. Use a geometric simulation inside Aura Pro if motion or obstructions could change the choice. The lift team checks the proposed route in the field.

Integration and native ownership

Import the client's schedule and preserve original calendars and revisions. Aura Pro can first publish an advisory comparison, then own selected forecast and resequencing workflows. Benchmark against a dated historical schedule while withholding later actuals. Test feasibility and forecast error separately. A GPU scene supplies geometric or physical evidence; project dates also require labor, procurement, approvals, and resource assumptions.
