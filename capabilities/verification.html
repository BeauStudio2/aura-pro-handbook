Verification and acceptance

What does this check establish, and what can proceed because of it?

Determine what a specific check establishes and who may accept the result.

In Mission Logic: The checker reports what a result establishes, and the responsible person accepts it for its supported scope. AP records what closes, what remains open, and which dependent actions are ready for authorized release. One person can both check and accept.

Approach

Tie acceptance to a specific requirement, location, revision, evidence method, and authorized reviewer. A passing dimensional check, a design review, and permission to commission have different scopes. A failed check calls for corrective work; a missing check calls for someone to perform it.

How it works

Receive the measured clearance and supplier dimensions. The reviewer records what they establish. After installation, collect the required installation or functional evidence; commissioning and occupancy each follow their applicable acceptance path. A later correction can reopen a dependent decision while preserving what was accepted and known on the earlier date.

Worked evidence and acceptance example → Follow an air-handler calculation into a scoped airflow check, distinguishing calculated compatibility, installation acceptance, integrated performance, and occupancy.

Technology choices

Acceptance establishes whether the agreed result was achieved. The value review separately examines its cost and benefit and what AP contributed. A passed check alone cannot establish a saving.

AP stores versioned criteria and check results in PostgreSQL, keeps evidence in access-controlled object storage, and uses deterministic comparisons for units and tolerances. It links client inspection records from the designated Procore project workflow. Alternatives include a native mobile checklist and reviewed inspector-signed document import. Recognition models supply draft extractions with source references for review. Supabase row-level security

Integration and native ownership

AP assembles review packets around the client’s existing acceptance process. When the client assigns a check to AP, the responsible reviewer records acceptance there. Keep external professional or agency decisions with the appropriate issuer and retain their references. Test wrong-floor evidence, an unperformed check, and a superseded acceptance. None should silently release adjoining work.
