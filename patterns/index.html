Where the patterns overlap

Related processes Shared reasoning The distinction that determines the next move
Restoration and continuous operations Compare observed function with an acceptable condition. A successful check can end a finite problem. In an operation it returns the affected part to service and observation continues.
Goal discovery and invention Let a discovery unsettle the existing plan. If the desired outcome is in question, ask the person what matters. If the means are in question, design a trial.
Design analysis and hierarchical planning Trace dependencies and compare consequences before commitment. Design analysis asks where one change reaches. Hierarchical planning works in both directions: the whole selects a detail, then the detail changes the whole forecast.
Transfer and experimental search Form a hypothesis and seek evidence that distinguishes explanations. Transfer asks whether evidence from elsewhere applies here. Experimentation creates a condition under which competing explanations predict different results.
Transfer and design analysis Use existing knowledge to anticipate effects before acting. Transfer tests comparability between cases. Design analysis tests the relationships among elements within the case.
Hierarchical planning and all six other processes Delegate a smaller reasoning problem to the appropriate process. A component may need restoration, a value decision, a design comparison, an analogy, an experiment, or sustained operation. Its result updates the model at the level where it has consequences.
