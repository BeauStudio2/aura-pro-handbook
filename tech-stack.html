Tech stack

Aura Pro connects the platforms a project uses so information recorded in one can guide work in another. Users keep control of their project data. They decide where the official record lives, who can use it, and what to share across systems.

Connect the work, then move functions deliberately

The construction client uses Procore, Raken and Sage today. AP starts with exports the team reviews: drawings and formal decisions from Procore, field measurements and reports from Raken, and financial records from Sage. AP connects their identities, revisions and meaning so a field finding can inform a forecast and reach the people who can act.

For the October tower, that means linking a clearance measurement to the drawing, the two installation sequences, the spending decision and the crew instruction. Using production rates from comparable jobs, AP forecasts each option’s cost and opening date and follows the approved sequence to the crew. People can trace what changed without rebuilding the same explanation in each tool. The official record for each function remains where the client designates it.

When clearance checks move into AP, the team gains direct capture tied to the question, visible review status, and automatic identification of the forecasts affected by a correction. The client’s responsible lead approves the transfer, open records and permissions are reconciled, and the next official entry has a stated home. Moving a workflow does not give AP new autonomy.

The client controls private material, sharing and portable exports. It can change platforms while retaining the permitted evidence and reasoning, keep a function in the tool that serves it best, and share only what another team needs. As AP takes on observation, planning and coordination, it supplies more of the complete system for projects of every scale, from a solo repair to a development involving thousands. Specialist tools remain replaceable.

Technical detail: records, handoffs and the tools behind the workflow

One tower decision

The foreman records a narrow opening in Raken. Aura Pro links that measurement to the issued drawing in Procore. The lift lead records the wider route’s checked dimensions with the field report in Raken. The scheduler opens the linked finding in Aura Pro and compares two ways to install the air handler.

  • Lift the assembled unit through two bays. Holding back a second façade panel and protecting the opening costs $4,000. The crew keeps the booked lift and ventilation tests. The opening forecast stays October 1.
  • Deliver the unit in sections and assemble it indoors. Assembly misses the booked tests. The next available round finishes October 21, moving the opening forecast to October 22.

Aura Pro flags the $4,000 request because the superintendent has $3,000 left. The executive receives the proposal with the two forecasts and the protection detail. The parties sign through the client’s agreed signing process, and the project manager records the executed change in Procore. With the detail reviewed and the change signed, the selected sequence goes through schedule approval and field release. Accounting posts the commitment in Sage. Aura Pro tracks the foreman’s acknowledgment and connects the posting to the signed change. Follow the tower decision →

What the client shares and keeps

The client chooses what each role can see. For the air-handler decision, the scheduler gets the checked opening dimensions, the drawing revision, the testing dates, and the $4,000 price for temporary protection. Those inputs are enough to compare the two sequences. Individual time cards stay in Raken, and wage rates and payroll stay within accounting. The foreman receives the approved instruction identifying which panels to hold and when to close them.

If the client switches platforms, it can take the decision and its evidence together. For this lift, that means the measurement and photo, the issued drawing revision, both forecasts with their assumptions and test dates, the signed change, the corresponding Sage commitment, and the crew’s acknowledged instruction. The export includes the files the client is entitled to transfer and an index linking each record by identifier, source, date, and revision. In the new platform, the scheduler can start at the approved two-bay lift and trace why the team still expected to open on October 1. That is the test for a usable export.

Who approves a handoff?

The client also reviews whether the change improves outcomes or reduces effort enough to justify transition and ongoing costs. The cost of obtaining useful intelligence makes preparation, training, support, and reconciliation part of that comparison.

The client names the person authorized to move each function. That person approves the scope, the switch date, and where the next official entry goes. Until that date, the team keeps using its current register.

For example, to move formal design questions, the client’s designated design lead approves the handoff. The project manager brings across open questions, pending responses, current drawing revisions, and reviewer permissions. The reviewer checks that a crew can retrieve the latest answer. From the switch date, the reviewer issues the next official answer in Aura Pro. The Procore question links to it.

Schedule and forecasts

Official record today
The scheduler maintains the approved working schedule in the client’s designated schedule register. At project setup, identify that register, its current revision, and who has authority to approve a new sequence.
What Aura Pro adds
The scheduler compares forecasts in Aura Pro against the same approved schedule. Each option shows the field evidence, testing slot, cost, and effect on opening day. The scheduler submits the recommended sequence for approval.
When it moves or stays
The client’s designated schedule authority approves moving the register. The scheduler transfers the approved revision, calendars, dependencies, and pending changes and checks that dates and approvals match. After the switch, the scheduler publishes the next approved revision in Aura Pro.

How the forecast becomes crew work. In this example, assume the project manager has delegated authority to approve a schedule revision. The project manager approves the two-bay sequence. The scheduler publishes that revision in the official register. The superintendent then issues the field instruction in Procore, naming the panels to hold and when to close them. The foreman acknowledges the revision and directs the crew. If crew instructions have also moved, the superintendent issues it and the foreman acknowledges it in Aura Pro.

Procore · Project decisions

Issued drawings

Official record today
The project manager keeps the issued sheets and revision history in Procore.
What Aura Pro adds
Links a measurement, affected work, and the scheduler’s forecast to the exact sheet revision.
When it moves or stays
Procore remains the drawing register in this design. Crews use the issued sheet there even after work instructions move.

Takeoffs and estimates

Official record today
The estimator reviews drawing quantities in Procore Estimating where enabled. Otherwise the estimator supplies a checked takeoff.
What Aura Pro adds
Matches design quantities to comparable production in Raken and posted costs in Sage. Shows how a revision changes the estimate.
When it moves or stays
The estimator keeps the official takeoff in the existing workflow. A later move requires the estimator to reproduce a reviewed package and its addendum in Aura Pro, trace changed quantities to the revised sheets, and explain differences from the accepted estimate. After handoff, the estimator enters the next revision in Aura Pro.

Design questions and answers

Official record today
The project manager logs the question against the drawing in Procore. The designated reviewer issues the technical answer.
What Aura Pro adds
Brings the drawing, field discrepancy, and decision deadline to the reviewer. Sends the answer to the people whose work depends on it.
When it moves or stays
The design lead authorizes the switch once the reviewer can revise an answer and the crew can find it. The next official response is issued in Aura Pro. Design handoff

Crew instructions

Official record today
The superintendent issues the approved instruction through Procore. The foreman acknowledges the revision.
What Aura Pro adds
Links the instruction to its approval and flags a missing acknowledgment or a superseded revision.
When it moves or stays
When the superintendent can issue and revise instructions and the foreman can acknowledge the current one, transfer open instructions and acknowledgments. The project then designates Aura Pro as the place to issue them.

Signed changes

Official record today
The project manager records the signed change in Procore. The authorized signatories commit the parties to its terms.
What Aura Pro adds
Routes the proposal to the signer and links the signed revision to the field instruction and the Sage posting.
When it moves or stays
Signed changes stay in Procore under this handoff. Moving field instructions or spending checks leaves signature authority with the signatories.

Raken · Field records

Measurements and photos

Official record today
The foreman records the reading, location, and photo in Raken.
What Aura Pro adds
Connects the observation to the drawing and planning assumption it tests. A copy of the same report in Procore counts as one observation.
When it moves or stays
In Aura Pro the foreman opens the scheduler’s request for that exact bay and drawing revision, then answers it on site with one reading and photo. The scheduler sees immediately which lift forecast needs another look, while the crew still has time to hold the next panel. That saves matching a daily report to the open question later. Move clearance checks when the crew can capture, correct, and retrieve them with author, date, and revision intact and the project accepts Aura Pro as their official record.

Daily reports and time cards

Official record today
The field team submits its daily reports and crew hours in Raken.
What Aura Pro adds
Uses dated reports to explain delays and matches hours to the activity and cost code used in the estimate.
When it moves or stays
These records stay in Raken when clearance checks move. The foreman can record the opening in Aura Pro and submit that day’s time cards in Raken.

Installed quantities

Official record today
Where Production Tracking is enabled, the crew records quantities installed by activity and cost code in Raken.
What Aura Pro adds
Pairs checked quantities with hours for the same work to calculate a production rate for the next plan.
When it moves or stays
Raken remains the source for installed quantities. If the crew’s records lack the quantity needed for a rate, the estimator asks for supporting field evidence.

Sage · Cost and accounting

Spending against an allowance

Official record today
The contractor’s delegation sets the limit. Procore holds signed changes and Sage holds posted commitments. Accounting reconciles them to establish what has been spent or committed.
What Aura Pro adds
Flags requests that appear to exceed the available balance and sends them to the authorized signer. A signed change that has yet to post can already use the allowance.
When it moves or stays
Aura Pro can reserve funds or block a request once opening balances and outstanding commitments are reconciled and every way to commit funds checks the same available balance before signature.

Job-cost postings and payroll

Official record today
Accounting posts commitments and actual costs in Sage by job and cost code and maintains the financial books. Payroll follows accounting’s approved process.
What Aura Pro adds
Matches a posting to the signed change, checks the posted amount against the forecast, and flags differences for the accountant.
When it moves or stays
Postings and financial books stay in Sage. Accounting retains payroll. Any transfer requires a separate accounting decision and reconciliation of opening balances, transactions, and reports.

Inside Aura Pro

The Aura Pro web app uses React, TypeScript, and TanStack. Supabase Auth identifies the participant. PostgreSQL stores project records and permissions, and private file storage holds evidence such as drawings and field photos.

The planning service uses SimPy to model how work unfolds over time and OR-Tools to compare schedules under crew, equipment, and calendar constraints. The scheduler reviews the assumptions and forecasts. Connections to the three client tools can start with reviewed exports and use supported interfaces where the client has access.
